About Us

From Bean to Cup, With Love

Founded in 2008, Brew Haven began as a tiny roastery with one drum roaster and a big dream. Today we work directly with farmers across Ethiopia, Colombia, and Sumatra to bring you ethically sourced beans roasted to perfection.

  • Direct-trade, single-origin beans
  • Small-batch roasted within 48 hours of shipping
  • Compostable packaging & carbon-neutral delivery
  • Award-winning head roasters
